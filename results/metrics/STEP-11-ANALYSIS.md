# Step 11 — Paired effects and failure cases

This exploratory trace review uses the complete 2,896-query MIRACL Arabic dev set. For each retriever it selects the five largest graded nDCG@10 wins, five largest losses, and ten ties sampled deterministically with seed 42.

The rank movements are factual associations, not proof that normalization caused a particular linguistic mechanism. Unjudged passages receive zero benchmark credit but are not human-confirmed negatives.
